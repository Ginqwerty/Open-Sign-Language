# IJCAI-25 Formatting Rules (IJCAI-25 论文格式要求)

## Page Layout (页面布局)

- **Paper Size (纸张尺寸)**:  
  8.5 inches × 11 inches (Letter).  
  (8.5英寸 × 11英寸，Letter 纸张格式。)

- **Margins (页边距)**:  
  - Left and Right (左右): 0.75 inches (0.75英寸)。  
  - Top (上边距):  
    - First Page (首页): 1.375 inches (1.375英寸)。  
    - Other Pages (其他页面): 0.75 inches (0.75英寸)。  
  - Bottom (下边距): 1.25 inches (1.25英寸)。

- **Two-Column Layout (双栏格式)**:  
  - Column Width (每栏宽度): 3.375 inches (3.375英寸)。  
  - Gap Between Columns (栏间距): 0.25 inches (0.25英寸)。

- **Column Height (栏高)**:  
  - First Page (首页): 6.625 inches (6.625英寸)。  
  - Other Pages (其他页面): 9 inches (9英寸)。


## Content Length (内容长度)

- **Submission Version (提交版本)**:  
  - Main Content: Maximum 7 pages. (正文最多7页。)  
  - References and Other Sections: Up to 2 additional pages. (参考文献及其他部分最多2页。)

- **Camera-Ready Version (终稿版本)**:  
  - Page limits may vary by track. (页数限制因分会要求而不同。)  
  - Some tracks allow purchasing extra pages. (部分分会允许购买额外页面。)


## Font and Typesetting (字体与排版)

- **Main Text (正文)**:  
  - Font (字体): Times Roman.  
  - Size (字体大小): 10 pt, with 1 pt leading (10 on 11).  
    (10 pt 字体，行距为1 pt。)  
  - Paragraph Indent (段首缩进): Indent all paragraphs except those following headings.  
    (段首缩进，标题后除外。)

- **Headings (标题)**:  
  - Section Headings: 12 pt, bold. (一级标题: 12 pt，加粗。)  
  - Subsection Headings: 11 pt, bold. (二级标题: 11 pt，加粗。)  
  - Subsubsection Headings: 10 pt, bold, unnumbered. (三级标题: 10 pt，加粗，无编号。)

- **Spacing Around Headings (标题间距)**:  
  - Section: 10 pt above, 4 pt below. (一级标题: 上10 pt，下4 pt。)  
  - Subsection: 8 pt above, 3 pt below. (二级标题: 上8 pt，下3 pt。)  
  - Subsubsection: 6 pt above. (三级标题: 上6 pt。)


## Submission and Camera-Ready Requirements (提交与终稿要求)

- **Anonymity (匿名性)**:  
  - Submission version must be anonymized. (提交版需匿名。)  
  - Camera-ready version must include author information. (终稿需提供作者信息。)

- **Line Numbers (行号)**:  
  - Enable line numbers for the submission version. (提交版需启用行号。)  
  - Disable line numbers for the camera-ready version. (终稿需禁用行号。)


## Abstract (摘要)

- **Placement (位置)**:  
  The abstract appears at the top of the first column.  
  (摘要放在首页第一栏顶部。)

- **Length (长度)**:  
  Maximum of 200 words. (最多200字。)

- **Style (样式)**:  
  - Title: "Abstract", bold, 12 pt, centered. (标题“Abstract”，12 pt 加粗，居中。)  
  - Text: Same font and size as the main text. (正文与主文一致。)


## Figures and Tables (插图与表格)

- **Figures (插图)**:  
  - Place figures at the top or bottom of the page. (插图应浮动置于页面顶部或底部。)  
  - Ensure high resolution (vector or at least 300 dpi). (图片需高分辨率，矢量格式或至少300 dpi。)  
  - Captions should be in 9 pt font, below the figure. (说明文字为9 pt，放置在插图下方。)

- **Tables (表格)**:  
  - Use the `booktabs` package for better styling. (推荐使用 `booktabs` 排版，优化表格样式。)  
  - Captions should be in 9 pt font, below the table. (说明文字为9 pt，放置在表格下方。)


## Equations (公式)

- **Formatting (排版)**:  
  - Split long equations into multiple lines if necessary. (将过长的公式拆分为多行。)  
  - Avoid using overly small fonts. (避免使用过小字体。)  
  - Equation numbers should match the main text font size (10 pt). (公式编号与正文字体一致，为10 pt。)


## Appendices (附录)

- **Purpose (目的)**:  
  Appendices are for supplementary content, such as detailed derivations, proofs, or additional results.  
  (附录用于补充正文内容，例如详细推导、证明或额外结果。)

- **Placement (位置)**:  
  Appendices must be placed after the main content and before the references.  
  (附录应放在正文之后、参考文献之前。)

- **Numbering (编号)**:  
  Use letters for numbering, e.g., Appendix A, Appendix B.  
  (附录使用字母编号，例如附录A、附录B。)

- **Formatting (格式)**:  
  Follow the same formatting rules as the main text.  
  (附录格式应与正文一致。)

- **Page Limit (页数限制)**:  
  Content in appendices counts toward the total page limit.  
  (附录内容计入总页数限制。)


## References (参考文献)

- **Citation Format (引用格式)**:  
  Use author-year format, e.g., ~\cite{author:year}.  
  (采用作者-年份格式，例如 ~\cite{author:year}。)

- **List Format (列表格式)**:  
  - Title: "References", unnumbered. (标题为“References”，无编号。)  
  - Use consistent formatting throughout. (格式保持一致。)  
  - Avoid citing unpublished work. (避免引用未公开发表的作品。)


## Ethical Statement and Acknowledgements (伦理声明与致谢)

- **Ethical Statement (伦理声明)**:  
  Optional. Discuss ethical implications of your research.  
  (可选部分，用于讨论研究的伦理影响。)

- **Acknowledgements (致谢)**:  
  Optional. Acknowledge contributions, funding, etc.  
  (可选部分，用于感谢合作、资助等支持。)
